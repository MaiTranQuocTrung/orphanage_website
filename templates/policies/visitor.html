We welcome visitors who support our mission while prioritizing the safety and wellbeing of our children.

Visiting Hours

  • Family visits: Saturdays and Sundays, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • Approved guests: By appointment only, Tuesday–Friday, 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM
  • Holiday visits: Special schedules announced monthly

Registration Requirements

  • All visitors must sign in at the front desk and present valid photo identification.
  • Family members must be on the approved visitor list maintained by the child's case worker.
  • First-time visitors require 48-hour advance notice and background verification.
  • Groups of 5 or more must schedule visits at least one week in advance.

During Your Visit

  • Visitors may use designated common areas; dormitory access requires staff escort.
  • Photography and video recording of children require written consent.
  • Gifts must be approved by staff and are subject to safety inspection.
  • Smoking and alcohol are prohibited anywhere on the premises.
