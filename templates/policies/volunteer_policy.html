Volunteers are essential to our mission. These policies ensure a safe, meaningful experience for both volunteers and children.

Eligibility & Screening

  • Volunteers must be at least 18 years old (16–17 with parental consent for limited roles).
  • All volunteers undergo background checks and reference verification.
  • A minimum commitment of 3 months and 4 hours per week is required for regular volunteers.
  • Volunteers working directly with children must complete child protection training.

Roles & Responsibilities

  • Tutoring: Academic support in math, Vietnamese, English, and sciences.
  • Activities: Sports, arts, music, and recreational programming.
  • Administrative: Office support, event planning, and fundraising.
  • Maintenance: Facility upkeep, gardening, and repairs.

Code of Conduct

  • Never be alone with a child behind closed doors.
  • Do not share personal contact information with children.
  • Report any concerns about a child's wellbeing immediately to staff.
  • Maintain confidentiality about children and shelter operations.
  • Follow all shelter rules and staff directives at all times.
