Zero Tolerance: Tuệ Quang Children has zero tolerance for any form of abuse, neglect, or exploitation of children.

Our Commitment

We are committed to creating and maintaining a safe environment where children are protected from physical, emotional, and sexual harm. This policy applies to all staff, volunteers, contractors, and visitors.

Prevention Measures

  • Mandatory child protection training for all staff and volunteers.
  • Comprehensive background checks before any individual works with children.
  • Two-adult rule: no adult is ever alone with a child unsupervised.
  • Secure facilities with controlled access and surveillance in common areas.
  • Regular safety audits and policy reviews conducted annually.

Reporting Procedure

  1. Any suspicion or disclosure of abuse must be reported immediately to the Child Protection Officer.
  2. The Officer will document the report and initiate an investigation within 24 hours.
  3. Serious cases are reported to local authorities and child welfare agencies as required by law.
  4. Whistleblower protections are in place for anyone reporting in good faith.

Support for Children

  • Trauma-informed counseling available for all affected children.
  • Children are informed of their rights and how to seek help.
  • Age-appropriate safety education provided regularly.
