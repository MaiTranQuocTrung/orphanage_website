Children Who Need Us Most

Tuệ Quang Children serves vulnerable young people who lack stable family support. Our beneficiaries include:

  • Orphaned or abandoned children
  • Children from families unable to provide basic care
  • At-risk youth referred by social services
  • Children recovering from abuse or neglect
  • Young people awaiting foster or adoptive placement
