Our intake process ensures every child receives appropriate assessment, documentation, and a warm welcome into the Tuệ Quang family.

1

Referral

Children are referred by social services, hospitals, community organizations, or legal guardians. All referrals are reviewed by our intake committee within 48 hours.

2

Initial Assessment

Our social workers conduct a comprehensive evaluation including medical history, educational background, emotional needs, and family circumstances.

3

Documentation

Required legal documents are collected and verified. This includes birth certificates, court orders (if applicable), and medical records.

4

Orientation & Welcome

New residents receive a facility tour, meet their caregivers and peers, and are assigned a buddy to help them adjust during their first weeks.

5

Care Plan Development

Within 30 days, an individualized care plan is created covering education, health, counseling, and long-term goals. Plans are reviewed quarterly.
